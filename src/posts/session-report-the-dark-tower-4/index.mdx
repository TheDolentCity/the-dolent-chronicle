*The Dark Tower* is a [Mythic Bastionland](https://chrismcdee.itch.io/mythic-bastionland) campaign of my own creation inspired by both [Browning's Poem](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/1597010/childe-roland-to-the-dark-tower-came) and [King's western/fantasy series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower_(series)). It relies on the core text in most ways, but has several notable changes, the most important of which is that rather than "seek the Myths" the knights all swore to ***SEEK THE DARK TOWER***.

There are five player characters:

- **Sir Dorza** the Moat Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Judoc** the Story Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Serana** the Dust Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Seamus** the True Knight (he/him).
- ~~**Sir Lucian** the Halo Knight (he/him).~~ Sir Lucian's player has stepped out of the campaign for now.

## Session Report

- **Snowfall:** The group awoke hungry and thirsty. They could tell more time than normal had passed. The mythical Tree's autumnal leaves were gone. The sky was grey and the ground cold. Snow began to fall. They weren't sure how long they were asleep, but Winter had come.
- **Exodus:** We had a flashback sequence for the past two weeks since Sir Seamus was away last session. He spent the time investigating Sheriff Reycarr's claim of nobility, and found much evidence to suspect it was a **LIE**. Seamus did convince Reycarr that the mythical Tree looming over Kirrik Tower was a danger. They proceeded to migrate the folk of Kirrik Tower several hexleagues to the West and Seamus prepared for his upcoming duel.
- **Dungeon:** The band of knights arrived back in Kirrik Tower to find most folk had fled to encampments in the West. Sir Judoc raced there to try and join Sir Seamus before his duel while Sir Serana and Sir Dorza investigated the empty tower. They found the cells empty, but there was an unlit stairway that descended deep below the tower. They moved down and found a cell in complete darkness with Cradoke, the Envoy inside. He was dead. Dehydration appeared to be the likely culprit. He was left here. The Knights freed his body and took it to be buried respectfully.
- **Duel:** Sir Judoc arrived at the encampment just in time to support Sir Seamus. The duel was to begin at sunset. Reycarr faced down Sir Seamus, when the latter suddenly declared Reycarr a **LIAR** and that he is not a noble, let alone the heir of Greywall. This struck Reycarr and turned the public against him. It also enabled Sir Seamus to use the True Knight's Punitive Strike ability (add +d10 to attack against a known liar and ignore armor). The two charged one another, Reycarr swinging a great battleaxe while Sir Seamus intended to deflect the blow with his shield and swing his mace into Reycarr's skull. As an ex-noble, Reycarr did have the ability to smite, but it did not matter here. Sir Seamus performed his smite and deny feats (saving for both), resulting in a total damage of 12 (ignoring armor) while Reycarr only managed to deal 5 damage. Since Sir Seamus has 18 vigour and 4 armor (I did not know this and holy shit what a tank) this did not even leave a scratch on him. Seamus's mace swung from below and connected with Reycarr's chin, obliterated his jawbone, and cracked the side of his head open from the force. With his dying breath Reycarr said, "Richard Redcloak".
- **Aftermath:** In the time that followed, Sir Seamus declared himself and the band of knights the interim council for Kirrik Tower. The Grey Season was upon them and they began to plan. The company were desperate for an easy way to ford the river that divided the realm, so Sir Serana spent the season managing the construction of a great bridge with the help of the people of Kirrik Tower. Sir Seamus, Dorza, and Judoc spent the winter traveling to nearby parts of the land to discover more of the realm, but we will leave what happened with them for the next session.
