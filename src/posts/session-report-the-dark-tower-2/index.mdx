*The Dark Tower* is a [Mythic Bastionland](https://chrismcdee.itch.io/mythic-bastionland) campaign of my own creation inspired by both [Browning's Poem](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/1597010/childe-roland-to-the-dark-tower-came) and [King's western/fantasy series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower_(series)). It relies on the core text in most ways, but has several notable changes, the most important of which is that rather than "seek the Myths" the knights all swore to ***SEEK THE DARK TOWER***.

There are five player characters:

- **Sir Dorza** the Moat Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Lucian** the Halo Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Judoc** the Story Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Serana** the Dust Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Seamus** the True Knight (he/him).

---

## Session Report

- **Downtime:** The knights decided to spend a few weeks at Castle Jericho performing downtime actions. Sir Seamus socialized with the upper nobility. Sir Judoc studied local history and stories, learning the tales of the Grey Company, the Giant's Tears, and the Spore Spewer Troll. Sir Serana spent time trying to teach the locals to fish, but they struggled somewhat with the speed and size of the mighty Moondrip river. Sir Dorza traveled to the barren fields north of Castle Jericho and dug ditches to provide irrigation. Sir Lucian joined a hunting party, and unbeknownst to the rest of the band, got lost in the mountains as a thick fog surrounded the group (_Sir Lucian's player could not attend the session_).
- **Journey to Kirrik:** The band of knights decided to leave Jericho and find Kirrik Tower. They knew the vassal there was poisoned and there was some political unrest. They crossed the Moondrip river via longship and later encountered a massive gorge. The rest of the day was spent traveling around the obstacle and its neighboring lake. At sunset, they spotted several armed men with leaf-like armor escorting a green bull towards Jericho.
- **The Green Bull:** Sir Seamus rode towards the green warriors and alerted them to the upcoming gorge. Their leader, Harbeger, thanked him and the two groups ate supper, where Harbeger spoke of finding the bull at the foot of a dead tree in a ruin to the south.
- **Fording the River:** The next day the band decided to help the bull and his entourage cross the wide Moondrip river. Sir Serana used his powers as the Dust Knight to repulse the river water long enough for the group to cross. He raced back to land before the river could swallow him too. The group then continued south towards Kirrik.
- **The Marsh and the Ruin:** In the marshlands north of Kirrik the band of knights discovered a ruined fort. Its stone walls burgeoned outwards and once inside the group could see why: a dead tree with massive roots that twisted outwards, knocking the fort's walls down. At its base they found an indentation, as if a bull had lain there. In a moment of inspiration, Sir Dorza struck the spot with his shovel and knocked some dirt aside, revealing a gigantic seed. Interested, the party collected the seed and were on their way, but not before Sir Dorza had a great night's sleep in the mud and moss of the marsh.
- **Arriving at Kirrik:** Covered in mud from head to toe, the band of knights approached Kirrik. At the sight of these armored warriors, the commoners in the outlying villages rushed inside their homes. After Sir Serana knocked on a door, a scared, old man answered, but was more confused when he learned they were knights. He explained that the Sheriff of Kirrik Tower, Reycarr, had instilled a curfew and hired many guards to enforce it. The old man also informed them that the Marshal suspected the Steward of poisoning the previous lord and murdered them. The Steward was later exonerated, and the Marshal was executed. The band suspected Reycarr was behind it all and went to speak with him.
- **Council with Reycarr:** Reycarr was hesitant and questioning of the band's motives at first, but decided to trust them a little as they did appear truly foreign and out of the loop. He insisted he was not behind the plot to poison Rowena and his curfew and guards were to instill order and peace. Sir Seamus gave an excellent monologue about hope and joy and how you use those to combat the paranoia and fear of the people. He then immediately undercut his point by belittling Reycarr, saying his poor bloodline wouldn't know any better and even called him a "hapless shit" to his face in a room full of armed guards.

The session ended with this insult. We'll pick up next time with Reycarr's reply. Very happy with the progression in this game. I was worried that exploration might stall out or no one would take initiative, but that isn't the case. During our post-game debrief, one player gave me the best compliment:
> It feels like the world is happening, and we're in it.
