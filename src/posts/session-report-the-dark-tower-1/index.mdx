*The Dark Tower* is a [Mythic Bastionland](https://chrismcdee.itch.io/mythic-bastionland) campaign of my own creation inspired by both [Browning's Poem](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/1597010/childe-roland-to-the-dark-tower-came) and [King's western/fantasy series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower_(series)). It relies on the core text in most ways, but has several notable changes, the most important of which is that rather than "seek the Myths" the knights all swore to ***SEEK THE DARK TOWER***.

There are five player characters:

- **Sir Dorza** the Moat Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Lucian** the Halo Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Judoc** the Story Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Serana** the Dust Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Seamus** the True Knight (he/him).

---

## Session Report

- **Quick History:** A flashback to the Knight's fallen realm of Camelot. Merlin disappeared, and in his absence a vision of the Dark Tower materialized in Arthur's court. So mighty and terrible was its visage that all the knights of the table swore an oath to pursue it. In the years that followed, most were killed or lost. Eventually, Mordred betrayed Arthur and the realm fell to ruin. Those few knights who survived sailed West, bound still to seek the Dark Tower.
- **The Knighting of Sir Dorza:** Arthur's council of seers was interrupted by an ambush from Mordred's forces. The Moat Knight, Dorza's father, died in the fighting. Dorza leapt into his father's muddy trench and continued fighting. In the aftermath, he was approached by the Brazen Seer. Dorza's pain was delicious to the seer and thus he knighted Dorza. The seer hopes to one day taste Dorza's pain again.
- **The Knighting of Sir Serana:** Serana was a fisherman who was caught unaware by a storm. An odd thing, considering how clear the horizon had been. He fought the sea, but his boat was sundered and his head struck by some debris. He began to sink, too weak to swim. Serana sunk down past where light can reach, and in that void he felt a presence. A vision of the future: knighthood, blood, war, a new realm, and of course at the center of it all the Tower. He surfaced from the ocean vomiting sea water, and knew that he had the honor of being a knight.
- **The Knighting of Sir Judoc:** Judoc was a legal scholar and pedant. His reputation as such earned him a visit by the Silvered Seer. The Seer confided he was not a seer at all, but Judoc would have none of it. They argued back and forth over the seer's nature for an entire day. The Silvered Seer was so frustrated that he punished Judoc with a forced knighthood before expelling him from his presence.
- **The Knighting of Sir Seamus:** The Rotted Seer was once a minor lord of Camelot, and Seamus was his son. Several minor lords attended a council where the Rotted Seer knighted his son. This was only to further the Rotted Seer's political machinations of course, but Seamus strived to make the most of it, binding himself to the truth going forward.
- **The Knighting of Sir Lucian:** Lucian was trained to be a knight since he was a young boy. The Painted Seer bid him visit his cave when Lucian was still young. Within that cave he saw paintings, visions of the grand life ahead of him. He visited again and again, but after he was of age and knighted, the paintings darkened. Greys, browns, and blacks mixed in with the greens and whites. Then Camelot fell. When Lucian visited the last time he understood that the vision was clear all along. Smudges were lost friends, spilled paint was dismemberment and blood, the ruin of the Camelot he just rode from. And black paint trickled in from the edges, hiding behind the brighter imagery, forming rivulets behind the painting, leading to a central black spear that rose from the floor to the roof of the cave. The Dark Tower loomed behind the vision of Lucian's life.
- **Arrival in a New Realm:** The knights all awoke at once from a seemingly magical slumber to find their longship on a wide river, sailing towards a castle on the horizon. There they encountered the Sheriff, Steward, and Envoy of Jericho Castle. All were hospitable, but visibly nervous hearing that the knights seek the Dark Tower. The knights were provided rooms and a map of the realm. The envoy Viralag spoke at great length with the band of knights and provided many details about the state of the realm, but underscored that the vassals were isolated from the seat of power and peace was beginning to crumble following the poisoning of the Vassal of Kirrik Tower, Rowena the Owl Knight.

The session ended with the knights deciding to spend a few weeks at Jericho to get their bearings, with a tentative plan to ford the river Moondrip and head South to Kirrik Tower.
