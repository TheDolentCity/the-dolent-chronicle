*The Dark Tower* is a [Mythic Bastionland](https://chrismcdee.itch.io/mythic-bastionland) campaign of my own creation inspired by both [Browning's Poem](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/1597010/childe-roland-to-the-dark-tower-came) and [King's western/fantasy series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower_(series)). It relies on the core text in most ways, but has several notable changes, the most important of which is that rather than "seek the Myths" the knights all swore to ***SEEK THE DARK TOWER***.

There are five player characters:

- **Sir Dorza** the Moat Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Lucian** the Halo Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Judoc** the Story Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Serana** the Dust Knight (he/him).
- **Sir Seamus** the True Knight (he/him).

## Session Report

- **Challenge:** Last session ended with Sir Seamus calling Sheriff Reycarr a "hapless shit". In response, Sheriff Reycarr stood, declared himself "heir of Greywall" and slapped Sir Seamus with a glove, challenging him to a duel. Sir Seamus accepted and everyone was escorted out. The duel would be in a fortnight, and until then Sir Seamus would be unable to leave the holding. (_Sir Seamus's player could not attend the session and this was all agreed upon beforehand over Discord_).
- **Investigation:** Suspicious of these hired guards, three of the knights spent the rest of the day drinking and merry making with them to try and glean some information. They learned that Kirrik Tower had not been sending its tithe to Castle Jericho and were instead paying these soldiers with this wealth. It also seemed like most of them found it to be an easy job and mostly driven by paranoia from Reycarr.
- **The Fish:** Sir Serana spent his day finding a nice fishing hole. Using his beckoning net, he found the prize fish of the pond. He returned to Kirrik Tower and sought audience with Reycarr where as a token of good faith, he offered the fish. Reycarr seemed genuinely touched by the act and promised he would not kill Sir Seamus in their duel.
- **The Growing Shadow:** The next day the band fished for rumors and whispers of myth around town, learning of a vine covered tower and a land where all the trees were burned stumps that continued to smoke to this day. They traveled west in the early morning sunlight with the shadow of Kirrik Tower in front of them. Then there was a horrible snapping and popping noise, and the shadow began to grow. Sharp spears of shadow that grew larger and larger, then branching spears from those spears, and so on, until the whole land before them was covered in the shadow of a massive tree.
- **The Bard:** The tree loomed a hexleague from Kirrik Tower and dominated the skyline, its trunk and branches disappearing into the autumn rain clouds. The band decided to head back, but not before finding someone to tell their tale. A drunk and very rotund bard left the tavern, but proved a magnificent flautist, so Tyack the Bard became their traveling companion. When they arrived back in Kirrik they promised the worried townsfolk they would investigate the tree and left to do so right away.
- **The Wandering Seer:** So large is this tree that its few remaining late autumn leaves were a potential looming catastrophe. When they fell, would they crush homes? Suspicious of who may have conjured the tree, the group went looking for signs of other folk around the tree and spotted a campfire. When they approached, they found a figure in hooded brown robes with their face wrapped in bandages. A boiling, well-spiced rabbit stew sat in a small cauldron over a fire. The figure asked if they would like some, but as he only answered their questions with questions, they began to grow uneasy. At last, the figure asked if they had "heard of the Wandering Seer?" And they said no, but decided that this figure must only be said seer and only able to speak in questions.
- **Palaver:** The group talked for a time, seeking insight from the seer, but his questions provided less concrete information than they would have liked. He did hint that the Tree came from a seed similar to the ones the knights carry, that King Gan was withholding information about why his quest for the Dark Tower ended, and that there was tale of a Lizard in the southwestern mountains. With the delicious stew in their bellies, the knights went to sleep.
- **The Grey Season:** The group awoke hungry and thirsty. They could tell more time than normal had passed. The Tree's autumnal leaves were gone. The sky was grey and the ground cold. Snow began to fall. They weren't sure how long they were asleep, but Winter had come.

The session ended with the players unclear on how much time had passed and worried they maybe missed Sir Seamus's duel. We'll find out if that's the case next week.
